UNDERSTANDING SECURITY

What Do Professional Security Guards Actually Do?

Security guards provide a visible and active layer of protection for properties, people, equipment, and events. Their responsibilities can include monitoring entrances, checking visitors, conducting patrols, observing surveillance systems, documenting incidents, and responding according to established procedures.

The right service depends on the property and the risks involved. A warehouse, apartment complex, office building, construction site, and public event can all require very different approaches. Good planning starts by identifying what needs protection and when the greatest risks occur.

SECURITY OPTIONS

Common Security Guard Services

Different situations call for different types of security. These are some of the services commonly used by businesses, property owners, event organizers, and contractors.

01

Armed Security

Armed security can be used in situations where a higher level of protection is required. Assignment requirements depend on the property, local regulations, and the specific risks involved.

02

Unarmed Security

Unarmed guards provide a visible security presence while handling duties such as access control, monitoring, reporting, visitor assistance, and routine patrols.

03

Mobile Patrols

Patrol services allow security personnel to check properties at scheduled intervals. They can be useful for large sites, commercial properties, parking areas, and locations that need regular monitoring.

04

Event Security

Events often require crowd monitoring, entrance control, parking oversight, and quick communication between security personnel and event staff.

05

Fire Watch

Fire watch personnel monitor designated areas when required fire protection systems are unavailable or when specific site conditions call for additional observation.

06

Construction Security

Construction sites can contain expensive equipment, materials, vehicles, and unfinished structures. Security services can help monitor access and identify suspicious activity.

PRACTICAL PROTECTION

Security Is About More Than Having Someone at the Door

A security guard's value is not limited to standing at an entrance. Depending on the assignment, guards can monitor activity, enforce access procedures, complete patrols, record observations, and communicate problems before they become larger incidents.

Clear instructions also matter. Guards need to understand property boundaries, restricted areas, emergency contacts, reporting procedures, and the responsibilities expected during each shift.

BEFORE HIRING

How to Choose the Right Security Service

Start with the actual problem instead of choosing a service simply because it sounds more comprehensive. Consider what is being protected, when the property is most vulnerable, how large the area is, and what guards will need to do during a normal shift.

It is also important to understand the company's experience with similar assignments. A guard service designed for an office building may operate very differently from one handling a construction site or large public event.

01

Identify the Risk

List the specific security problems you are trying to address.

02

Define the Coverage

Determine the locations, hours, and responsibilities that need coverage.

03

Compare Providers

Review relevant experience, service options, staffing, and communication procedures.

04

Set Clear Expectations

Make sure duties, reporting, escalation, and site procedures are clearly understood.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between armed and unarmed security?

Armed guards carry authorized firearms as part of their assignment, while unarmed guards do not. The appropriate option depends on the property, risks, regulations, and responsibilities of the assignment.

What does a mobile security patrol do?

A mobile patrol involves security personnel traveling through designated areas to check property conditions, observe activity, and report issues according to the assignment's procedures.

Can security guards work at construction sites?

Yes. Construction security assignments can include monitoring entrances, checking the property, observing equipment and materials, and documenting unusual activity.

Is security useful for private events?

Security personnel can help with entrance monitoring, crowd control, parking areas, restricted zones, and communication during private or public events.
